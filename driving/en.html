Privacy Policy

Driving

Last updated: 2026-09-29

No registration

You do not need to register. We do not collect your name, phone number or email address.

Your data stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Crash reports and usage statistics

The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics via Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. They contain no personal information. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Store purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Telegram version

The Telegram version stores only your Telegram user ID, product ID, unlock status, payment ID and payment time — this keeps your purchase unlocked. Telegram Stars payments are processed by Telegram. To request a refund, use the bot's /paysupport command.

AI-generated explanations

Explanations are generated by AI and reviewed by people.

Independence

This app is made by an independent developer and is not affiliated with any government agency.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
